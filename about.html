About this map

NZ Free Parking helps you explore published parking locations and areas across Auckland and understand the information available for each one.

What the map shows

The map covers Auckland using Auckland Transport parking locations, paid-parking and loading-zone areas, and Auckland Council car-park surface footprints. Read each feature’s type and source details to check what it represents.

Dots show locations; filled shapes show published areas. AT polygons show mapped paid or loading zones. Council polygons show car-park surfaces, which may be only part of a car park. Neither a footprint nor a payment zone proves that every spot inside it is available or permitted for parking. Unmapped places are not automatically free.

Using the map

  1. Explore a location. Click or tap a point or area to see its source details. A tap pins the details until you close them. Where mapped features overlap, their details are listed together. Select a list entry to highlight it and zoom to its boundary; use Show more to see further entries.
  2. Open it in Maps. Point-location details include a link to your device’s maps service. A recorded point may not be a car park entrance. Area details do not invent a navigation destination from a polygon centre.
  3. Find parking near you. Choose Locate me and allow location access. The map centres on your position and the list sorts by approximate distance to the mapped feature. An area containing your position has zero distance; this is not a driving-route estimate. Choose the button again to stop location updates.
  4. Change the background. On mobile, open Map options to find the base map and theme controls. The Base map selector offers OpenStreetMap Streets, Humanitarian and Terrain (OpenTopoMap). These backgrounds do not change the parking information.
  5. Choose parking layers. In Map options, expand Parking layers to show or hide points, AT paid zones, AT loading zones and Council footprints. Counts show the loaded records. Layer choices are saved in this browser.
  6. Choose a theme. The theme button cycles through Auto, Light and Dark. Auto follows your device’s colour preference.

What the colours mean

Area colours identify the source category. Point-marker colours are inferred from their published descriptions. Neither is a live check of permission or space availability.

Where the information comes from

Parking data currently shown

The map queries Auckland Transport’s Car Parking service, linked from the data.govt.nz catalogue. It includes location points, descriptions, addresses and some space counts. The published dataset is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.

The app fetches parking data on opening and every 15 minutes while running. The “Updated” time records a fetch attempt, not when the council last checked a location. A source-failure message means that attempt did not fully succeed, and earlier data may remain visible.

Official parking prices and hours

Visit Auckland Transport: on-street and open-air parking for published charging schedules, operating details and car-park information. This is an important companion to the location data shown on this map.

Official polygon overlays currently shown

These three layers are enabled by default and can be switched off individually. Polygon rings, holes and disconnected parts are preserved. Large datasets are fetched in batches to avoid service record limits. Records without drawable geometry are omitted and reported beside the parking list; the displayed layer counts include only drawable features.

  • AT Paid Parking Zones — mapped zone polygons and zone IDs, without tariff schedules.
  • AT Loading Zones — polygon areas with restriction and operating-time fields.
  • Auckland Council Park Structure Hard Surface — car-park surface footprints among other parks assets. A footprint does not establish fees, access or parking rules.

Additional source not yet shown

AT Mobility Parking Facilities offers more structured mobility restrictions. The separate Car Parking location layer currently supplies the points.

AT data is credited to Auckland Transport. Car-park footprint data is credited to Auckland Council. Follow each source link for its metadata and use terms.

Base maps and credits

  • OpenStreetMap Streets: © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • Humanitarian: OpenStreetMap data, a style by the Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team, and tiles from OpenStreetMap France.
  • Terrain: OpenStreetMap and SRTM data, with map styling by OpenTopoMap under CC BY-SA.

Map display uses MapLibre GL JS for GPU-accelerated rendering. If it cannot start or its graphics context is lost, the app switches to Leaflet as a backup. The active renderer is shown below the map. The selected base map’s source credit is also shown on the map itself.

Important limits

  • Coverage is incomplete. This is not a full inventory of every public parking space or street restriction in Auckland.
  • Rule schedules are incomplete. Location-point labels are inferred from descriptions. Loading zones have some schedule fields, but the combined sources do not provide a complete schedule of prices, permitted hours, holidays or temporary restrictions. A colour cannot confirm the rule for a particular moment.
  • Space counts are not live occupancy. A listed capacity or availability figure does not guarantee an empty space when you arrive.
  • Published details can lag changes. Follow the signs and road markings at the location, including temporary restrictions.

For current official information, check on-street and open-air parking details or central Auckland car-park information. Report source-data issues through Auckland Transport.

Location and preferences

Your location is requested when you choose Locate me. The browser uses that position to centre the map and sort nearby locations. You can stop updates with the same button or change location permission in your browser settings.

Your base map, layer visibility and theme choices are saved in this browser. Loading the map and parking information makes requests to the external map providers and the Auckland Transport and Auckland Council data services. Following an “Open in Maps” link sends the selected parking location to the maps service.
